Relational ER Diagram Entities & Normalization Schema (Slide 13)

Normalized 3NF relational design connecting stakeholders, products, parts, materials, repairs, and circular end-of-life events.

STAKEHOLDER (ISA)
  • 🔑 Stakeholder_ID (PK)
  • • Name, Phone, Email, Address
  • • MANUFACTURER (Country, Reg_No)
  • • OWNER (Owner_Type)
  • • SERVICE_CENTRE (License)
  • • RECYCLING_FACILITY (Permit, Cap)
PRODUCT_MODEL & ITEM
  • 🔑 Model_ID (PK), Manufacturer_ID (FK)
  • • Model_Number, Name, Type
  • • Energy_Rating, Carbon_Footprint
  • 🔑 Product_ID (PK), Model_ID (FK)
  • • Serial_Number, Mfg_Date
  • • Status, State_of_Health, Owner_ID
COMPONENT & MATERIAL
  • 🔑 Component_ID (PK), Product_ID (FK)
  • • Component_Type, Serial_Number, Status
  • 🔑 Material_ID (PK), Name
  • • Recyclability, Hazardous, RoHS
  • 🔗 COMPONENT_MATERIAL (Is Made Of)
  • • Quantity, Unit
CIRCULAR LIFECYCLE
  • 🔑 REPAIR (Cost, Date, Description)
  • 🔑 OWNERSHIP_HISTORY (Transfers)
  • 🔑 RECYCLING_EVENT (Intake, Decommission)
  • 🔑 SALVAGE (Grade A/B/C Reusable Parts)
  • 🔑 RECOVERY (Raw Materials Yield, Purity)
  • 🔑 LIFECYCLE_EVENT (Audit Timeline)
